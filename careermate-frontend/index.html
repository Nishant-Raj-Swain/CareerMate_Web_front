THE COMMAND MENU

Sixteen commands. One chat window.

Everything CareerMate can do lives behind a slash command. Scroll, drag or hover to spin the wheel.

⚡
/news

Today's top tech news

Three quick headline cards worth knowing before your next interview or standup.

🏆
/competitions

Latest competitions

Hackathons, case comps and coding contests worth your weekend.

💼
/internships

Latest internships

Fresh openings, so you apply the week they're posted, not the week they close.

📝
/practice

Practice quiz

Rapid-fire questions on any topic, to see where you actually stand.

🎯
/ats <role>

ATS score by role

Check how a screening bot rates your resume, e.g. /ats Devops.

📄
/chat_pdf

Chat with your PDF

Upload a PDF and ask questions about it in plain language.

📚
/books

Top career & tech books

Discover the best reads to level up your career and skills.

📋
/pyq

Previous year questions

Practice real past papers and find your weak spots.

📄
/send

Upload your resume

Upload or review your saved resume for the bot to work with.

🔍
/job_search

Open job roles

Search live openings that match your skills, with a match score.

📺
/utube_notes

YouTube to notes

Drop a YouTube link and get detailed, structured notes back.

🗺️
/roadmap <topic>

Learning roadmap

An official or AI-built, step-by-step roadmap for any skill.

💬
/assistance

Ask anything career

Stuck on an offer, cover letter or pivot? Just ask.

🚫
/cancel

Exit or stop

Bail out of a running quiz or operation instantly.

🗑️
/delete

Clear your session

Wipe saved session data and your uploaded resume.

💬
/feedback

Share feedback

Report an issue, give a suggestion or tell us what you think.
